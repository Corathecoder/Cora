Buses, shared jeeps, local trains and that last stretch on foot can take you almost anywhere around Pune — if you know how they fit together. LocalT exists to make that clear.

Our story

It started on a 6 am bus

LocalT was started by Bhoomi Pandey, a student of Avasara Academy, in her 11th grade. It grew out of her own frustrating journeys on a PMPML bus from her home to school and back. She really hated how the bus almost never came on time, and how crowded it was — even at 6 am in the morning.

That experience led her to create this project and start building a community of local travellers.

She alone could not solve the problem, but together we could make it the best for everyone.

The problem

LocalT began around one problem: making local and public transport easier to understand for travellers — which bus to take, where to board, where to get down, delays, crowding and the practical last-mile connection.
